FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about planning your tour in Sri Lanka.

When is the best time of year to visit Sri Lanka?

Sri Lanka offers pleasant weather year-round! November through April is ideal for Galle, Mirissa, and the Hill Country. May through September is great for East Coast beaches like Trincomalee.

Can we customize any of the itineraries?

Yes, all our packages are 100% customizable. You can adjust days, hotel tiers, or add specific stops matching your flight times.

What is included in the package price?

Packages include private air-conditioned vehicle with fuel, experienced tourist chauffeur guide, hotel stays with breakfast, and mentioned entry/safari permits.

How do we book and confirm our trip?

Simply submit the inquiry form below or message us directly on WhatsApp. We will send a customized proposal and lock in your dates.
